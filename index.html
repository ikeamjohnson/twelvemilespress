About the press

Twelve Miles Press publishes literature, criticism and translation.

Founded by a comparative literature scholar, Twelve Miles Press champions works across borders, genres, and traditions that illuminate interior lives, ancestral philosophies, and hidden spiritual currents. The press holds that no tradition is off-limits, no voice is marginal, and the deepest truths live where disciplines meet.

Its books are for every reader, and its doors are open to writers, translators and scholars of every background.

Publishing

Small by design

Each year Twelve Miles Press publishes a limited number of books across the three areas below. The list ranges across the world's literatures: Black and African diasporic, Mediterranean and Middle Eastern, Asian, Indigenous, and beyond.

Translation

Literature and thought carried into English, and critical editions that return overlooked works to readers with fresh scholarly framing.

Criticism and thought

Rigorous, readable criticism and philosophy on ancestral wisdom, spiritual traditions and the meeting of disciplines, written for classrooms and curious readers alike.

Literature

Fiction, poetry and literary nonfiction that cross borders and illuminate interior lives.

Submissions · Opening December 2026

Coming soon: your work

Twelve Miles Press will open to submissions in December 2026. Writers, translators and scholars of every background and at every stage, from first-time writers to established voices, are welcome. Please don't send work before then. The guidelines below show what to send.

The press seeks work in its three areas: translations and critical editions, criticism and thought, and literature (fiction, poetry and literary nonfiction). Translations from any language and bilingual work are welcome.

Creative work

Fiction, poetry and literary nonfiction. Send a cover letter with a short bio and a one-page synopsis, plus the first three chapters (about 50 pages), or 10 to 15 poems.

Criticism and thought

Send a cover letter and a proposal: an overview of the project, a chapter outline, its intended readers (classrooms, scholars, general readers), a sample chapter, and a short bio. A CV is welcome but not required, and independent scholars are encouraged to submit.

Translations and critical editions

Send a cover letter describing the original work and its author, the rights status of the original, and a sample of 20 to 30 pages with the corresponding original text.

How to submit

Email your work to editor@twelvemilespress.org with the subject line Submission: Title, Your Name. Attach your files as PDF or Word documents.

Good to know

  • Selection is highly competitive, and each acceptance reflects a strong fit with the press.
  • There is no reading fee, and no agent is needed.
  • First-time, emerging, independent and non-academic writers are warmly welcome.
  • Simultaneous submissions are welcome; please send word if your work is accepted elsewhere.
  • Every submission is read with care, with a response within four to eight weeks.
  • If you need an accommodation to submit, such as a different format or more time, write to the editor to arrange it.

Masthead

Editor and publisher

Ikea M. Johnson, Ph.D.

Twelve Miles Press was founded by Ikea M. Johnson, a scholar of comparative literature who holds a Ph.D. from Louisiana State University. She has served as Executive Editor of the Journal of Comparative Literature and Aesthetics since 2020 and teaches literature, philosophy and film at a New England university. Her work spans African American literature, Kemetic and Buddhist philosophy, and Afrofuturism.
